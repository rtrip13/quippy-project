# UNLABELED interface

A college field notebook: clear information, useful experiments, occasional warmth.

- Use the shared palette in `src/design/tokens.ts`. Paper and ink are the default; evergreen identifies actions, selection, and progress. Reserve rust for warnings or destructive actions.
- Use the system font. Screen titles are 32/38, body text 16/24, secondary text 14/21, and compact metadata no smaller than 12. Prefer regular body copy and semibold labels.
- Use sentence case and concrete names: Explore majors, Saved majors, Compare majors. Keep UNLABELED as the wordmark. Avoid slogans and repeated reassurance before a task.
- Group ordinary content with space and fine rules. Cards are for a distinct next action or selectable object, not every section. No raised bottom edges or decorative shadows.
- Keep one primary action prominent. Secondary actions use text or a light outline; do not style routine navigation as a warning.
- Scout belongs in onboarding and meaningful celebrations, not routine narration across the main tabs.
- Use consistent navigation icons. Keep labels visible, targets at least 44 points, and controls tall enough for wrapping text.
- Explain local storage in the data section. Show saving feedback while pending and retain visible failure feedback. Do not permanently reserve space for a successful-save banner.
- Keep source provenance available. Put detailed qualifications beside the data they describe, without hiding important limitations or inventing certainty.
- Check long program names, empty and selected states, enlarged text, and keyboard behavior when changing layouts. Preserve persistence, program selection, reflection, and advisor-sharing behavior.
